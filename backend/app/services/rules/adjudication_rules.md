# Adjudication Rules

1.  **Waiting Period Validation**: Claims for certain illnesses are not covered within the first 30 days of the policy. Maternity claims are not covered within the first 270 days.
2.  **Exclusion Validation**: Claims for cosmetic surgery and dental treatments are not covered.
3.  **Sub-limit Validation**: The consultation fee is capped at 1000. Room rent is capped at 5000 per day.
4.  **Co-pay Application**: A 10% co-pay is applicable on the final approved amount.
5.  **Fraud Detection**: If the claim amount is greater than 50000, flag for manual review.
